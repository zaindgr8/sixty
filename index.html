FAQ

Questions, answered in under 60 seconds.

Is it the official WhatsApp Business API?

Yes. Sixty runs on the official WhatsApp Business Platform, so your number stays compliant and verified. We handle the Meta setup for you and you keep your number.

Will my customers know they're talking to AI?

Sixty writes like a real member of your team: short, warm, and in the customer's own language. You choose how it introduces itself, and it hands over to a human whenever the customer asks for one.

What does "60 seconds" actually mean?

The first reply goes out within seconds. Within 60 seconds of the lead's message, Sixty has replied, asked your qualifying questions, saved the lead to your CRM and alerted your team about hot leads. Setup itself takes about 7 days.

Does it reply in Arabic and other languages?

Yes. Sixty speaks English, Arabic and 40+ other languages, and automatically replies in the language your customer writes or speaks in.

Which CRMs does it work with?

HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, GoHighLevel, Pipedrive and Google Sheets, plus almost any other system through Zapier, n8n or webhooks. If your CRM has an API, we can connect it.

What happens when the AI can't answer?

It hands the chat to your team with a short summary and the reason, and lets the customer know someone will follow up. You can also take over any conversation yourself with one click.

How is the AI Call Agent priced?

On your call volume, voices and integrations. Tell us what you need on a 15-minute call and we'll send a fixed monthly quote within 24 hours.

Is my data safe?

Your conversations are stored securely and only your team can access them. We never use your customers' data to market to them, and you can export or delete it anytime.
