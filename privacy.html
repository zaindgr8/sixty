Sixty

Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Applies to sixty.devmatesolutions.com, the Sixty dashboard and the Sixty Meta app ("Sixty by DevMate").

In short: Sixty is a service that businesses use to answer their own customers on WhatsApp, Instagram and phone with an AI agent. We process messages only to provide that service to the business that connected the account. We never sell personal data or use it for advertising, and you can ask us to delete it at any time (see Data deletion).

1. Who we are

Sixty is a product of DevMate Solutions, with offices in Dubai (United Arab Emirates), New York and Muscat. For privacy questions, contact us at management@devmatesolutions.com.

When a business (our client) uses Sixty to talk to its customers, the client decides why and how those customers' data is used and is the data controller. DevMate Solutions acts as the client's service provider (data processor) for that data. For our own website visitors and the people who sign up for Sixty, we are the controller.

WhoData

We do not access a person's private account data beyond their conversations with the client.

3. How we use it

  • To deliver messages between a client and its customers, and show them in the client's Sixty inbox.
  • To let the client's AI agent reply, qualify the enquiry, book a next step and hand the conversation to a human when needed.
  • To score and summarise leads for the client's team, and send them alerts.
  • To operate, secure and improve Sixty, provide support, and meet legal obligations.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, build profiles for other businesses, or use one client's customer conversations to serve another client. We do not use data received through Meta platforms for any purpose other than providing the messaging service to the client who connected the account.

4. AI processing

Replies, lead scores and summaries are generated with AI models, currently Google Gemini (Google LLC). Conversation content is sent to the model only to produce the reply or analysis. It is processed under the provider's API terms, which do not allow it to be used to train their public models. Clients can see and override every AI reply, and can switch the AI off for any conversation.

5. Who we share it with

We share data only with service providers that help us run Sixty, under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger): to send and receive messages.
  • Twilio: WhatsApp messaging and phone numbers.
  • Retell AI: AI voice calls.
  • Google (Gemini API): AI replies and analysis.
  • Supabase and Vercel: secure database and hosting.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of people or our service. Clients may connect their own tools (for example a CRM); data sent there is governed by the client's agreement with that provider.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS). Connection keys and access tokens for WhatsApp, Instagram and voice providers are encrypted at rest. Each client's data is kept separate, access is limited to authorised staff and the client's own team, and passwords are stored only as salted hashes.

7. How long we keep it

We keep conversation and lead data for as long as the client's account is active, or until the client deletes it. When a client disconnects an Instagram or WhatsApp account, we stop receiving new data from it immediately and delete the stored access token. When a client closes their account, we delete their data within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep records for longer. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (including under the UAE Personal Data Protection Law and the GDPR), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, or object to how it is used. If you contacted a business that uses Sixty, you can ask that business directly or write to us and we will help. To have your data deleted, follow the steps on our Data deletion page. We respond within 30 days.

9. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States and the European Union. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

10. Children

Sixty is a business service and is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. We will post the new version here with a new effective date and, for significant changes, notify clients by email.
