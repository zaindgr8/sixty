Data deletion

Effective 20 October 2026 · How to remove your data from Sixty by DevMate Solutions.

Quickest way: email management@devmatesolutions.com with the subject “Data deletion request”. We confirm within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

If you messaged a business on WhatsApp or Instagram

Sixty is used by businesses to answer their customers. If you chatted with, called, or commented on a business that uses Sixty and want your data removed:

  1. Email management@devmatesolutions.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Include the phone number (WhatsApp or calls) and/or the Instagram username you used, and the name of the business, if you know it.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request from that number or account, so we only delete your own data.

We will delete your messages, call recordings and transcripts, lead details and profile information from Sixty, tell the business it was removed, and send you a confirmation with a reference number.

Remove Sixty’s access from Instagram

You can also stop an Instagram account from sharing data with Sixty at any time: in Instagram open Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites, choose Sixty by DevMate and tap Remove. On Facebook use Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

If you are a Sixty client (business account)

  • Disconnect a channel: in the Sixty dashboard go to Workspace settings → Connections and click Disconnect on Instagram, or remove your WhatsApp details. We stop receiving data immediately and delete the stored access token.
  • Delete a contact: ask us by email and we will remove that customer’s conversations and lead data.
  • Close your workspace: email us from the owner’s address. We delete all of the workspace’s conversations, leads, documents, settings and team accounts within 30 days.

What we may keep

We keep only what the law requires, such as invoices and records of the deletion request itself. Backups are overwritten within a further 30 days. See our Privacy Policy for details.
